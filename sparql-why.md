# What is SPARQL and Why It Matters in Health and Life Sciences

*Presenter: Jerven Bolleman — 20–30 min*

SPARQL is the W3C-standard query language for the Resource Description
Framework (RDF) and plays a central role in semantic data integration across
healthcare and life sciences.

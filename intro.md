# Learning SPARQL By Example

This half-day tutorial at SWAT4HCLS 2026 introduces SPARQL through a
hands-on, learning-by-example approach. Participants will create, document,
and publish SPARQL queries using the
[sparql-examples](https://github.com/sib-swiss/sparql-examples) format and
contribute to [SPARQL-Fest](https://sparql-fest.github.io), a shared
platform for showcasing workshop outputs.
